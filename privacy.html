Legal

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

The short version
  • We collect what's needed to run your account and the AI features.
  • The desktop app has no ads or ad trackers. Google shows ads only on our download and unlock pages, personalised only if you allow it.
  • Your projects stay on your computer. The app sends only the files a request needs.
  • We don't keep the text of your AI requests or the answers after they're delivered. We keep only token counts, per day and per billing month.
  • When the AI is busy, your request may wait in a short line. Its place in line is kept for a few minutes at most.
  • We never sell your personal data.
  • Optional cookies stay off until you allow them. You can delete your account at any time.

1. Who we are

MyCraftingAI ("we", "us") provides the MyCraftingAI desktop app, the website mycraftingai.com and the online account service. For personal data processed through the Service, we are the data controller. Questions about this policy go to contact@mycraftingai.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, username, a securely hashed password (never the password itself), and your Google account ID if you sign in with Google. We also keep your plan, whether your email is verified, your 2-step verification setting, your email preferences, and which version of the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy you agreed to and when.
  • Sign-in and security data: for each active session, the type of device or app, the IP address and when it was last used. We also keep short-lived records of failed sign-ins and sign-in codes to stop abuse and lock out attackers.
  • Usage counts: how many tokens and requests your account uses each day and in each billing month, to apply your plan's allowance, track your top-up tokens and show your usage.
  • AI requests: the instructions you type and the project context the app attaches, such as file names and the source files relevant to that request. In the 3D Studio and Texture Studio this includes your description of a mob or texture and the structure of its model (bone names, sizes and positions). When you use the Version Converter, the source code and data files of the mod you choose are sent in batches to be converted (its folder is read on your computer and never changed; assets such as textures are copied locally and not sent). These pass through our servers to our AI model provider to produce the answer. We don't store the content of requests or answers afterwards.
  • AI queue records: when many people use the AI at once, a request can wait in line before it starts. While it waits or runs, we keep a short-lived record of it: a random request ID, your account ID and plan, its place in line, how long it has waited and how often it was retried. The app uses this to show you your place in line. The record contains no request content and is deleted when the request finishes, within 15 minutes at most. We also keep overall counters (for example how many requests waited) that are not linked to anyone.
  • Billing status: from our payment provider we receive your plan, subscription status, the date and time your subscription started (which sets your billing cycle), renewal and end dates, and a link to your customer portal. We keep whether your account has already received its first-month allowance. We never receive your full card number.
  • Token top-ups: when you buy tokens, we keep the order number, the number of tokens, the price and processing fee, when you bought them, when they expire and how many you have used. Top-ups our support team adds for you are recorded the same way, with a short note.
  • Ad unlocks (Free plan): when you unlock a tool by watching an ad, we keep a short-lived record linked to your account: a random single-use ticket, its status and timing, how many seconds the unlock page was open in front (sent every two seconds while it is visible), how many unlocks you used today and when your unlock ends. These records delete themselves within 48 hours.
  • Messages you send us: support requests and the messages you send with the sales and support forms on our contact page: your email address, your message and, if you choose to give them, your name and company. They are delivered to our contact@ or support@ inbox, used only to answer you, and kept for as long as needed to handle your request (normally no longer than 24 months). The forms are protected against abuse by counting messages per IP address and email address for a short time.
  • Technical data: when your browser or the app connects to us, our servers briefly log standard request data (IP address, time, requested address, response code) for security and troubleshooting.

3. What we don't collect

  • We don't upload your project folder. Projects, chat history, settings and crash logs from the desktop app stay on your computer unless you choose to send them to us.
  • We don't sell personal data, and we don't share your account details, prompts or projects with advertisers.
  • The desktop app contains no ads and no advertising trackers. Advertising cookies can only appear on our download and unlock pages, as described in Advertising.
  • We don't use your projects or prompts to advertise, and we don't sell them.

4. How we use your data

  • to create and secure your account, sign you in, and send codes and security notices;
  • to provide the AI features and apply your daily or monthly allowance, your top-up tokens and fair use;
  • to keep the AI service stable at busy times: requests wait in line, are retried automatically, and plans with priority access are served first;
  • to manage subscriptions and token top-ups together with our payment provider, and to credit purchased tokens to your account;
  • to answer support requests and tell you about important changes to the Service;
  • to send product news, only if you leave that option on;
  • to show ads on our download and unlock pages, and to grant ad unlocks fairly (daily limits and fraud checks);
  • to detect, investigate and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents;
  • to meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

5. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Contract: running your account, the AI features, your subscription and the token top-ups you buy, including the priority your plan gives you when requests wait in line.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the Service secure and stable (including managing busy periods), preventing abuse (including ad-unlock fraud), fixing problems, improving the Service, and funding the Free plan with non-personalised ads. We balance these interests against your rights.
  • Consent: optional cookies, personalised advertising and product news emails. Where the law requires consent for advertising cookies, we ask for it first. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: keeping records required by tax, accounting and other laws.

6. Who processes your data

We use a small number of service providers. Each receives only what it needs, and each is bound to protect it:

  • AI model providers: third-party providers of the language models that generate answers. They receive only the request content needed to produce an answer.
  • Lemon Squeezy: our payment provider and merchant of record for subscriptions and token top-ups. They process checkout, payments, invoices and tax under their own privacy policy. To credit a top-up to the right account, we send them your account ID and the number of tokens with the checkout; they send these back to us with the payment confirmation.
  • Google: if you choose "Continue with Google", to confirm your identity. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which shares your IP address with Google. Google AdSense and Google Ad Manager serve the ads on our download and unlock pages (see Advertising).
  • Hosting and email providers: run our servers, database backups and outgoing email.
  • GitHub: hosts the website and app downloads. Downloading the app or checking for updates connects to their servers.
  • Software library repositories: when you build or export a mod, when the Version Converter downloads the official template for a loader and version, or when the 3D Studio looks up the right version of a library such as GeckoLib, your computer connects to public repositories (for example Maven Central, the NeoForge, Fabric and Forge repositories, and the GeckoLib repository hosted by Cloudsmith). Like any website, they see your IP address and which files are downloaded. We send them no account details.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a merger or acquisition. If that happens, this policy continues to apply to your data.

6.1 Advertising

Ads help keep the Free plan free. They appear only on our download page and on the unlock page the app opens for ad unlocks. The desktop app, the account page, sign-in, pricing and these legal pages show no ads.

  • Who serves them. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Your choice. Personalised ads are used only if you switch on "Personalised advertising" in Cookie settings. Otherwise we ask Google for non-personalised ads, which are based on context such as the page and your general location, but Google may still use cookies or similar technology for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention.
  • Opting out. You can opt out of personalised advertising from Google at Google Ads Settings, and from many other vendors at aboutads.info or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu.
  • How Google uses data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through its ad cookies.
  • What advertisers don't get. We never pass your name, email address, account details, prompts or projects to Google or advertisers. The unlock page works with a random single-use ticket, not your identity.
  • Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through a consent message before advertising cookies are used, as the law requires.

7. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States and China for AI processing. Where the law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses, and we limit what is sent to what each request needs.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account data: while your account is open. When you delete your account, it's deleted from our live systems straight away, and from backups as they're replaced.
  • Sessions: until you sign out, the session expires, or you end it from the account page.
  • Security records: sign-in codes expire within minutes. Failed sign-in counters are cleared after a short time. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.
  • Ad unlock records: deleted automatically within 48 hours. Unlock links expire after 30 minutes.
  • AI queue records: deleted when the request finishes, and within 15 minutes at most.
  • Usage counts: while your account is open.
  • Billing records: as long as tax and accounting law requires, which is usually several years. Our payment provider keeps its own records.
  • Support messages: as long as needed to help you, and up to two years after.

9. Security

Our safeguards include encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), password hashing with bcrypt, optional 2-step verification, encryption of the AI provider keys we hold (they are never shown again once stored), sign-in rate limits and lockouts, session revocation, and staff access limited to what's needed. Every change made in our administration tools is recorded in an internal activity log. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we'll notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Emails

You choose which emails you get on your account page. You can turn off product news, plan and billing updates, and new sign-in alerts there. Some messages are needed to keep your account working and safe, so they're always sent: verification and sign-in codes, critical security notices, payment problems, and replies to your support requests.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or California law), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy in a portable format;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data (you can delete your account yourself on the account page);
  • object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email contact@mycraftingai.com from your account's email address. We'll answer within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first. We don't sell or "share" personal data for cross-context advertising, and we won't treat you differently for using your rights.

13. Children

The Service isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data or show them personalised ads. In some countries, people under 16 need a parent's or guardian's consent. If you think a child has given us personal data without the consent that's required, contact us and we'll delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy when our practices change. The date at the top shows the latest version. When we publish a new version, we'll show you a short summary of what changed and ask you to review and accept it the next time you sign in on the website or open the app; for significant changes we'll also tell you by email. Until you accept, AI features are paused on your account, and you can delete your account at any time if you don't agree.
