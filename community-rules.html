The Workshop

Community rules

Effective 17 November 2026

The short version
  • Be kind and helpful. Everyone was a beginner once.
  • Share only your own work, or work you're allowed to share, and say which licence applies.
  • Never upload malware or anything that hides what it does.
  • Keep it safe for a young audience: no adult, hateful or graphic content.
  • No spam. Promote your mods in Showcase and Mod Releases.
  • Report problems instead of fighting about them.

These rules are part of our Terms of Service.

1. The spirit of The Workshop

The Workshop is a place for people who make Minecraft mods with MyCraftingAI to show what they built, learn from each other and get unstuck. Many members are young or new to modding. Treat the community like a friendly workshop: help out, give useful feedback and leave the place better than you found it.

2. Be kind

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, insults or pile-ons, in public or in private messages.
  • No hate speech or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or age.
  • Criticise work, not people. "The texture is hard to read at 16x16" helps; "this is trash" doesn't.
  • Don't impersonate other members, the MyCraftingAI team, Mojang or Microsoft. Team members have a "Team" badge; moderators have a "Mod" badge.

3. Share safely

  • No malware. Never upload viruses, token loggers, "stealers", miners, obfuscated or packed code meant to hide what a mod does, or anything that harms players' computers, accounts or servers. This leads to an immediate ban.
  • No cheats for other people's servers, hacked clients, exploits, duplication glitches for public servers, or stolen or shared accounts.
  • Describe what your mod does, which Minecraft version and loader it needs, and anything it changes outside the game.
  • Upload the file itself. Don't link to downloads hidden behind shorteners, ad walls or "verification" sites.
  • Even with these rules, remember that mods run code on your computer. Only install mods from people you trust and keep backups of your worlds.

4. Respect people's work

  • Post only mods, code, textures, models, sounds and screenshots you made, or that you have permission to share. Credit everyone who helped.
  • Don't re-upload other people's mods or resource packs, even with changes, unless their licence allows it. Say which licence your own uploads use (for example MIT, All Rights Reserved or CC BY).
  • Don't share Minecraft's own game files or other copyrighted material you don't own.
  • If you believe something infringes your rights, report it or email contact@mycraftingai.com.

5. Keep it clean

  • No sexual or sexually suggestive content, nudity, or graphic violence and gore.
  • No content that promotes self-harm, drugs, weapons trading, gambling or illegal activity.
  • Keep language reasonable. Mild words are fine; slurs and constant swearing aren't.

6. No spam

  • Don't post the same thing in several spaces, flood comments, or send unwanted messages to lots of members.
  • No advertising for unrelated products or services, referral links, or vote and karma manipulation (including using several accounts).
  • New accounts have lower limits on posts and links for their first day. Limits relax automatically.

7. Use the right space

  • Showcase - mods, builds, mobs and textures you made.
  • Mod Releases - finished mods with a download, changelog and supported versions.
  • Help & Bugs - build errors and crashes. Include the error text or log, your Minecraft version and loader.
  • Textures & Models - pixel art, resource packs, 3D models and animations.
  • Tutorials - guides and walkthroughs.
  • Ideas & Feedback - mod ideas and feedback on MyCraftingAI.
  • Announcements - news from the MyCraftingAI team (only the team posts here; everyone can comment).

8. Privacy and private messages

  • Don't post personal information - yours or anyone else's - such as real names, addresses, phone numbers, school names, passwords or account details.
  • Never ask other members for passwords, sign-in codes or payment details. The MyCraftingAI team will never ask for them.
  • Private messages follow the same rules as public posts. You can block anyone, and turn off messages from your community settings.
  • Moderators can't read private messages unless one is reported. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

9. Reporting

See something that breaks the rules? Use Report on the post, comment, message or profile. Reports are confidential: the member you report isn't told who reported them. Content that several members report may be hidden automatically until a moderator reviews it. Please don't report content just because you disagree with it.

10. What moderators do

Moderators are members of the MyCraftingAI team and trusted volunteers. Depending on how serious a problem is, they may:

  • remove a post, comment or message, and tell the author why;
  • lock a thread or pin an important post;
  • mute an account for a while (it can still read, but not post, comment, vote or message);
  • ban an account from The Workshop, or for the most serious cases (such as malware) close the MyCraftingAI account under our Terms.

A Workshop restriction doesn't affect your projects, the desktop app or your paid plan. If you think a decision was wrong, write to support@mycraftingai.com and a person will review it.
