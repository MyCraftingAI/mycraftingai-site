Install in three steps

  1. Open MyCraftingAI-Setup.exe from your Downloads folder and follow the installer.
  2. Start MyCraftingAI and sign in, or create a free account.
  3. Pick a folder for your mod, describe what you want and press Send.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time you run a new release. Choose More info, check the publisher and file name, then Run anyway.

System requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • 8 GB RAM recommended, so Minecraft can run next to the app
  • Free disk space for the app and your projects (mod development kits use a few GB)
  • An internet connection for the AI assistant and your account
  • Java is downloaded automatically when a project needs it

Free, with no ads in the app

The desktop app never shows ads. On the Free plan, Build & Run and Texture Studio can be unlocked with a short ad on our website, or with any paid plan.
